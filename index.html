Common Orthodontic Problems That May Require Treatment

Orthodontic issues can develop during childhood or become more noticeable in adulthood. Some common conditions that may benefit from orthodontic treatment include:

Crowded Teeth

When there is insufficient space in the jaw, teeth may overlap or become twisted. Orthodontic treatment can gradually move teeth into better alignment.

Gaps Between Teeth

Spaces between teeth can affect smile appearance and sometimes allow food particles to become trapped. Braces or other orthodontic appliances may help close unwanted gaps.

Overbite

An overbite occurs when the upper front teeth extend too far over the lower front teeth. Appropriate treatment can help improve bite alignment.

Underbite

An underbite occurs when the lower front teeth sit in front of the upper front teeth. Orthodontic assessment can determine the most appropriate correction options.

Crossbite

A crossbite happens when some upper teeth sit inside the lower teeth when biting down. Treatment may help improve how the teeth fit together.

Orthodontic Treatment Options for Different Needs

Modern orthodontics offers several treatment options to address different alignment concerns. The right approach depends on the patient's age, dental condition, treatment goals, and professional assessment.

Traditional Metal Braces

Traditional metal braces are a widely used orthodontic solution for correcting mild to complex alignment problems. They use brackets and wires to apply controlled pressure, gradually moving teeth into their desired positions. Regular appointments allow the orthodontist to monitor progress and make necessary adjustments.

Clear Braces

Clear braces offer an alternative for patients who prefer a less noticeable orthodontic option. They work similarly to traditional braces but use tooth-coloured or transparent brackets that blend more naturally with the teeth.

Clear Aligners

Clear aligners are removable trays designed to move teeth gradually. They can be a convenient option for suitable patients who want a discreet orthodontic treatment. Consistent wear according to the orthodontist's instructions is important for achieving the expected results.

Maintaining Your Smile During Orthodontic Treatment

Good oral hygiene is essential while wearing braces or clear aligners. Brushing twice daily, flossing regularly, and following professional cleaning recommendations can help keep teeth and gums healthy.

Patients with braces should avoid particularly hard, sticky, or chewy foods that may damage brackets and wires. Those using removable aligners should follow their prescribed wear schedule and clean their trays as instructed.

Regular orthodontic appointments are equally important because they allow the treatment team to monitor tooth movement and make adjustments when needed.
